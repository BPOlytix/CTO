# Management Report Template

This document defines the structure and logic for the automated management reports generated by Accrue AI, sourced from Xero data.

## 1. Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement

The P&L provides a summary of revenues, costs, and expenses incurred during a specific period.

### 1.1 Structure
*   **Operating Revenue**: Total sales from primary business activities.
*   **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: Direct costs attributable to the production of the goods sold or services provided.
*   **Gross Profit**: Operating Revenue - COGS.
*   **Operating Expenses (OpEx)**:
    *   Sales & Marketing
    *   General & Administrative (G&A)
    *   Research & Development (R&D)
*   **EBITDA**: Gross Profit - OpEx (before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization).
*   **Depreciation & Amortization**
*   **Operating Income (EBIT)**: EBITDA - Depreciation & Amortization.
*   **Interest Expense/Income**
*   **Net Income**: EBIT - Interest - Taxes.

### 1.2 Xero Logic
*   Fetch via `GET /Reports/ProfitAndLoss`.
*   Map Xero account types: `REVENUE`, `SALES` -> Operating Revenue.
*   Map Xero account types: `DIRECTCOSTS` -> COGS.
*   Map Xero account types: `EXPENSE`, `OVERHEADS` -> OpEx.

## 2. Balance Sheet

The Balance Sheet provides a snapshot of the company's financial position at a specific point in time.

### 2.1 Structure
*   **Assets**
    *   **Current Assets**: Cash, Accounts Receivable, Inventory, Prepaid Expenses.
    *   **Non-Current Assets**: Property, Plant, & Equipment (PP&E), Intangible Assets.
*   **Liabilities**
    *   **Current Liabilities**: Accounts Payable, Accrued Liabilities, Deferred Revenue, Short-term Debt.
    *   **Long-term Liabilities**: Long-term Debt, Deferred Tax Liabilities.
*   **Equity**
    *   Common Stock
    *   Retained Earnings

### 2.2 Xero Logic
*   Fetch via `GET /Reports/BalanceSheet`.
*   Current Assets: Sum of accounts with type `BANK`, `CURRENT`.
*   Current Liabilities: Sum of accounts with type `CURRLIAB`.
*   Non-Current Assets: Sum of accounts with type `FIXED`.

## 3. Key Financial Ratios

Ratios are calculated to provide deeper insights into liquidity, profitability, and operational efficiency.

### 3.1 Liquidity Ratios
*   **Current Ratio**: Measures the ability to pay short-term obligations.
    *   *Formula*: `Total Current Assets / Total Current Liabilities`
*   **Quick Ratio (Acid Test)**: Measures ability to meet short-term obligations with most liquid assets.
    *   *Formula*: `(Total Current Assets - Inventory) / Total Current Liabilities`

### 3.2 Profitability Ratios
*   **Gross Margin (%)**: Percentage of revenue exceeding COGS.
    *   *Formula*: `(Total Revenue - COGS) / Total Revenue * 100`
*   **Net Profit Margin (%)**: Percentage of revenue remaining after all expenses.
    *   *Formula*: `Net Income / Total Revenue * 100`
*   **Operating Margin (%)**:
    *   *Formula*: `Operating Income / Total Revenue * 100`

### 3.3 Efficiency Ratios
*   **AR Turnover**: How quickly the company collects payments.
    *   *Formula*: `Net Credit Sales / Average Accounts Receivable`
*   **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**:
    *   *Formula*: `(Average Accounts Receivable / Total Credit Sales) * Number of Days in Period`

---

## 4. Report Metadata & Logic

*   **Reporting Period**: Default is Monthly (Month-to-Date and Year-to-Date).
*   **Comparison**: Compare against previous month and same month previous year (YoY).
*   **Accuracy Check**: Ensure that `Net Income` on P&L matches the change in `Retained Earnings` on the Balance Sheet (accounting for dividends).
*   **GAAP Compliance Note**: Reports must reflect accrual-basis accounting as defined in the `gaap_mapping_rules.md`.
